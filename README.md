## 🌟 Why Camera Plexus?

Unlike traditional virtual background extensions that only blur or replace the wall behind you, **Camera Plexus integrates directly into Chromium's camera stream pipeline (`Insertable Streams`)**. 

Everyone on your call — whether on **Google Meet, Facebook Messenger, OmeTV, Discord, or Zoom Web** — sees all your glowing hand effects, drawing in the air, and gestures **in real time on your actual camera feed without any extra software required**!

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🎯 1. Selective Finger Targeting (Zero Fist Clutter!)
* **Intelligent Per-Finger Tracking:** When your hand is closed in a fist, zero nodes or messy cluster lines appear!
* **Target Only What You Open:** Whichever fingers you extend — whether it's an **"L" / Gun shape** (thumb + index), **Pointing** (index only), **Peace sign** (index + middle), **Rock horns** (index + pinky), or **Open hand** — ONLY those active extended fingers light up with neon nodes and laser energy rays radiating directly from your palm!
* **Silky Smooth Dissolve:** Closed fingers fade out smoothly in milliseconds with continuous exponential decay, giving a high-tech Iron Man holographic feel.

### 🖐️ 2. Anime & Superhero Power FX (Live Gestures)
Trigger jaw-dropping superhero and anime visual effects simply by gesturing with your hands:
* ⚡ **Chidori / Lightning Fist (Naruto):** Clench your hand into a closed fist — dynamic branching electric lightning arcs, intense cyan glow, and crackling high-voltage sparks envelop your fist accompanied by an authentic electric crackle sound!
* 🕸️ **Spider-Man Web Shoot (Marvel):** Flash the iconic Spider-Man sign (🤟) — shoots a high-speed conical web tracer directly toward the camera! Neon webbing splats and sticks right on your screen glass with sagging polygonal rings, dewdrop nodes, and dripping strands with a synthesized "THWIP!" sound.
* 🔥 **Super Saiyan Aura (Dragon Ball Z):** Strike the DBZ power-up pose by spreading both arms wide — surging golden flame pillars, rising Ki particles, screen-edge golden aura vignette, and crackling Super Saiyan 2 lightning erupt around you with a resonant power hum!
* ⚔️ **Laser Sword / Lightsaber (Star Wars):** Press your Index and Middle fingers together (sword hand grip) — a brilliant lightsaber blade projects forward from your fingertips with a blinding white-hot laser core, neon energy bloom, hilt emitter ring, humming sound, and flying plasma embers!
* 🖐️ **Doctor Strange Mystic Shield:** Open your palm facing the camera to generate a rotating ancient mystic mandala shield with concentric fiery circles and rotating geometric runes.
* 🔮 **Two-Hand Plasma Energy Ball (Rasengan):** Hold your palms facing each other to create a crackling, swirling plasma energy sphere with live electric arcs bridging your hands!
* ✌️ **Peace / Victory Sign:** Spawns a celebratory burst of fluttering multi-colored confetti and glowing stars.
* 👍 **Thumbs Up:** Launches golden fireworks and glowing floating thumbs-up emblems.
* 🫶 **Heart Sign:** Form a heart with both hands or finger-pinch to release floating neon love hearts that drift upwards with romantic glow.

### ✏️ 3. Air Canvas (Draw in the Air)
* **Air Drawing:** Point your index finger in the air (or pinch thumb and index together) to paint smooth glowing neon ribbon strokes in 3D/2D space!
* **Smooth Bézier Curves:** Custom quadratic Bézier interpolation ensures your air handwriting is silky-smooth with zero jagged edges.
* **Custom Colors & Size:** Pick any neon color and brush size from 1px to 25px.
* **1-Click Wipe:** Clear your air canvas anytime with the on-screen trash button.

### 🎨 4. 10 Stunning Visual Presets
* 🌈 **Rainbow Mode:** Dynamic RGB spectrum cycle that flows seamlessly like gaming RGB lighting.
* 🧙 **Doctor Strange:** Fiery amber, mystic gold, and glowing orange embers.
* 🔥 **Fire:** Blazing red, fiery orange, and rising heat sparks.
* 🌆 **Cyberpunk:** Neon pink, electric cyan, and deep purple lasers.
* 💻 **Matrix:** Digital hacker green code stream and falling glyphs.
* 💜 **Violet, ❄️ Ice, ⚡ Ember, 💖 Neon, ⚪ Mono**

### 🔊 5. Audio-Reactive Pulse & Sci-Fi Sounds
* **Voice & Beat Reactivity:** When you speak or play music, the plexus lines and glow dynamically pulse in thickness and brightness to your voice volume!
* **Synthesized Audio Chimes:** Built-in subtle sci-fi sound effects when turning on/off or triggering gestures (synthesized via Web Audio API, 100% offline).

### ⚡ 6. Sleek On-Screen Floating HUD
* An interactive glassmorphism control menu floats directly on your video call screen.
* Change themes, toggle gestures, activate Air Draw, or wipe the canvas in 1 click **without opening browser popups or leaving your call**!
* **Ghost Menu:** The floating HUD is rendered in your local DOM — **remote callers only see your cool video effects, never the menu!**
* Can be dragged anywhere on your screen or minimized into a small glowing floating icon (`✨`).

### 🏎️ 7. 60 FPS Ultra-Smooth Performance
* **Continuous Exponential Motion Interpolation:** Eliminates AI detection micro-stutter for fluid, silky 60fps tracking.
* **Direct GPU Acceleration:** Front-buffer desynchronized hardware rendering.
* **Batched Path Strokes:** Reduces canvas draw calls by ~90%.
* **🌱 Eco Mode:** Built-in battery and CPU/GPU saver for low-end laptops.

### 🔒 8. 100% Privacy & Offline AI
* Powered by bundled on-device **Google MediaPipe Vision AI**.
* **Zero network calls:** Your webcam feed, microphone, and hand tracking never leave your local computer.

## 📥 How to Install (Takes 30 Seconds!)

### Step 1: Download the Extension
* Click the green **Code** button above and select **Download ZIP**, then extract it.  
  *(Or use the pre-packaged zip in the releases)*

### Step 2: Load into Chrome / Edge / Brave / Opera
1. Open your browser and go to:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions
   ```
2. Turn **ON** the **Developer mode** toggle in the top-right corner.
3. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
4. Select the extracted `meet-plexus` folder.

🎉 **That's it! Camera Plexus is installed and ready.**

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Open **Google Meet**, **Messenger**, or **OmeTV** and turn on your camera.
2. **Turn On / Off:**
   - 👏 **Double-Clap** your hands in front of the camera (two claps within a second).
   - ⌨️ Press **`Alt + Shift + P`** on your keyboard.
   - ⚡ Click **Power** on the on-screen floating HUD or the extension toolbar icon.
3. **Trigger Gestures:**
   - ✌️ Show a Peace sign for confetti!
   - 👍 Show a Thumbs Up for fireworks!
   - 🫶 Make a Heart shape with your hands!
   - 🖐️ Hold up an open palm for the Doctor Strange mystic shield!
   - 🔮 Bring two palms close together for the Plasma Energy Ball!
4. **Draw in the Air:**
   - Click **✏️ Air Draw** on the floating menu and point your index finger to write in the air!

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

```
meet-plexus/
├── manifest.json         # Manifest V3 configuration (Universal URL matching)
├── detector/             # Sandboxed MediaPipe HandLandmarker & Segmenter iframe
├── src/
│   ├── hook.js           # getUserMedia WebRTC stream interception
│   ├── pipeline.js       # Insertable Streams (MediaStreamTrackProcessor/Generator)
│   ├── engine.js         # Central coordinator (tracker, audio, renderer, state)
│   ├── plexus.js         # Batched GPU Canvas 2D plexus web renderer
│   ├── gestures.js       # AI gesture detector (Peace, Heart, Shield, Ball, Air Draw)
│   ├── hud.js            # Glassmorphism on-screen floating control bar
│   ├── audio-clap.js     # Web Audio API transient & FFT volume analyzer
│   ├── lighting.js       # Ambient light spill and silhouette rim-lighting
│   ├── particles.js      # Particle systems (shards, dissolving dust, confetti)
│   ├── settings.js       # Reactive settings model with presets
│   ├── status.js         # Non-intrusive status pill notifications
│   └── tracker.js        # Communication bridge to detector iframe
└── popup/                # Chrome toolbar settings dashboard
```
